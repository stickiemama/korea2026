The cards

Record

The biggest tank in Korea

The main tank, "The Sea of Jeju," holds about 5.3 million litres of water, roughly two Olympic swimming pools. The whole aquarium holds about twice that. It's the largest aquarium in South Korea.

Scary but gentle

Sand tiger sharks

Those sharks with jagged teeth sticking out look terrifying, but sand tiger sharks are usually calm and slow. They have a strange trick: they gulp air at the surface and hold it in their stomach so they can hover in the water without sinking.

Gross but true

Sharks that eat their siblings

Sand tiger shark babies grow inside their mother, and the biggest, strongest embryos eat their smaller brothers and sisters before they're born. Usually only one or two survive to be born, already big and tough.

Giant

A fish that has to breathe air

The arapaima from the Amazon is one of the largest freshwater fish in the world and can grow about 3 m long. It breathes air and has to come to the surface every 10 to 20 minutes, or it drowns. Its scales are so tough that piranhas struggle to bite through them.

Trick question

Shark or ray?

The giant guitarfish has a shark-like tail and fins, but it's actually a ray. Look for its gills: rays have them underneath their body, while sharks have them on the sides of the head.

Weird but true

Fish that switch

Many groupers start life as females and can change into males later on. The biggest kinds of grouper can grow over 2 m long and swallow their prey whole.

Look closer

Seal or sea lion?

Sea lions have little ear flaps and can turn their back flippers forward to "walk" on land. Seals have just a hole for an ear and wriggle along on their bellies like a caterpillar. Sea lions are also much louder.

Jeju

Haenyeo in the tank

Aqua Planet holds demonstrations of Jeju's haenyeo, the women divers, inside the main tank. Real haenyeo dive with no oxygen tank, holding their breath for a minute or two and diving 10 m or deeper for shellfish, octopus and seaweed.

Behind the scenes

A hospital for sea animals

Aqua Planet has South Korea's first medical centre just for marine animals. Some of the sharks and rays in the tanks were also bred there rather than caught in the wild.

Big debate

Where is the whale shark?

The main tank once held whale sharks, the biggest fish on Earth. One whale shark and a manta ray died, and many people argued that animals that size should not be kept in tanks. The surviving whale shark was tagged and released into the sea. What do you think aquariums should keep?
