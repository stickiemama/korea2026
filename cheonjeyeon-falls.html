Part 2

The cards

Name

The Pond of the Emperor of Heaven

Cheonjeyeon means "Pond of the Emperor of Heaven." It's one of Jeju's three famous waterfalls, along with Cheonjiyeon and Jeongbang, which are both on our list for Friday.

Legend

Seven nymphs at night

Legend says seven nymphs came down from heaven at night to bathe in the pond. The arched Seonimgyo Bridge above the falls is decorated with them. People also believed that standing under the falls on the 15th day of the seventh lunar month could cure illness. Swimming is not allowed today.

Remember: the seven nymphs carved on the bridge.
Weird but true

The waterfall that needs rain

The first fall drops 22 m into a pool 21 m deep, as deep as a seven-storey building is tall. But it only flows after heavy rain. Most of the time water seeps through the volcanic rock instead. Thanks to Typhoon Champi, we may have seen it at its best.

Protected

A rare warm forest

The gorge is a pocket of warm-climate forest, protected as a Natural Monument since 1993. Rare plants grow in its rock cracks, including the whisk fern, a strange leafless plant whose relatives have existed for hundreds of millions of years.

Volcano

Why Jeju has waterfalls by the sea

Jeju's lava is full of cracks, so rain usually sinks straight underground. It reappears near the coast, where it hits harder rock layers and pours over the cliffs. That's why Jeju's big waterfalls are close to the sea, not up on the mountain.
