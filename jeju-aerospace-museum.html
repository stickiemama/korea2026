The cards

Before you go

Hall of planes

Real aircraft, not models

The main hall holds real military and civilian aircraft, many flown by Korea's air force. Look for the F-4 Phantom II, the F-86 Sabre from the Korean War era, the F-5, and even a Soviet-designed MiG-19, plus a replica of the Wright brothers' first plane.

Spot: the oldest and the newest aircraft in the hall.
History

The jets of the Korean War

The Korean War (1950–53) saw some of the first big battles between jet fighters, American F-86 Sabres against Soviet-built MiGs. You'll find both types in the museum. Link it to our Korea, China & Japan page.

Space

Korea goes to space

In 2008 Yi So-yeon became the first Korean in space, taking specially made space kimchi with her. In 2022 Korea's own Nuri rocket reached orbit, and its first lunar orbiter, Danuri, set off for the Moon.

Nearby history

An old airfield in the fields

About 20 minutes away near Moseulpo, the Japanese navy built Alddreu Airfield in the 1930s. Concrete aircraft hangars from World War II still stand in the farmland there.

Science

Why planes fly

A wing is shaped and angled so that air flowing over it pushes it upward. That's lift. Engines provide thrust to push forward, while gravity and air resistance pull the other way. Flying means balancing all four forces.
