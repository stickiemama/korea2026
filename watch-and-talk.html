The one we started

Film · Netflix · 2025

Good News

A dark comedy about the real 1970 hijacking of a Japanese plane by young radicals who wanted to fly to North Korea, and the South Korean officials who disguised Gimpo Airport as Pyongyang to trick them. Satire, so the officials are exaggerated, but the hijacking and the fake-Pyongyang trick really happened.

Talk about: why would South Korea go to such lengths to stop a plane reaching the North in 1970? And we went through Gimpo on Day 1.
Part 1

Set on Jeju

Series · Netflix · 2025

When Life Gives You Tangerines

A family saga set on Jeju from the 1950s onwards. The heroine's mother is a haenyeo, and the story follows how island life changes over decades. Moving and funny, and full of places like the ones we're seeing.

Talk about: what has changed most on Jeju since the 1950s, judging by what we see now?
Series · Netflix · 2022

Our Blues

Linked stories about people in a Jeju fishing town: market vendors, a truck seller, divers and old friends. A good picture of everyday island life. Some storylines deal with grown-up themes, so check a few episodes first.

Talk about: which character would run a stall at Olle Market?
Documentary · Apple TV+ · 2024

The Last of the Sea Women

A documentary about Jeju's haenyeo divers, many in their 70s and 80s, and their fight to protect their seas and their way of life.

Talk about: would you want to keep a tradition alive if it meant such hard, dangerous work?

History on screen

These link to the Korea, China & Japan page. Some have battle or protest violence, so check the ratings.

Film · 2014

The Admiral: Roaring Currents

Admiral Yi Sun-sin's famous battle at Myeongnyang in 1597, when he faced a Japanese fleet of over 130 ships with just 13. A huge hit in Korea. Its follow-up, Hansan: Rising Dragon (2022), shows an earlier battle.

Talk about: we'll see his statue on Gwanghwamun Square. Why is he still Korea's greatest hero?
Series · Netflix · 2018

Mr. Sunshine

Set in the early 1900s as Japan tightens its grip on Korea. A Korean-born American officer returns to Joseon and gets caught up with independence fighters. Beautiful costumes and old Seoul settings.

Talk about: how would you have felt living through those years, before Japan took over in 1910?
Film · 2021

Escape from Mogadishu

Based on a true story: in 1991, North and South Korean diplomats trapped in Somalia's civil war had to work together to escape, even though their countries were enemies.

Talk about: what does this story say about the people of North and South, compared with their governments?
Film · 2017

A Taxi Driver

Based on real events: a Seoul taxi driver drives a German journalist to the city of Gwangju in 1980, where the military government was violently crushing pro-democracy protests. The footage the journalist smuggled out showed the world what happened.

Talk about: South Korea only became a full democracy in 1987. How does that change how you see modern Seoul?

Fun picks

Animated film · Netflix · 2025

KPop Demon Hunters

A K-pop girl group who secretly fight demons. Look out for Seoul landmarks like N Seoul Tower and Bukchon's rooftops, and a tiger and magpie straight out of traditional Korean folk paintings.

Talk about: spot the Seoul places we'll visit, and look for the tiger and magpie in museum paintings.
Documentary series · Netflix · 2019

Street Food: Asia, "Seoul"

One episode of this food series is all about Gwangjang Market, where we're planning our last Seoul dinner, and the women who have run its stalls for decades.

Talk about: which stall from the episode do we want to find?
Film · 2006

The Host

A monster bursts out of the Han River and grabs a girl from the riverbank, and her chaotic family set out to rescue her. Scary in places, funny in others, by the director of Parasite.

Talk about: we're cycling along the Han on Wed 4 Nov. Keep an eye on the water.
