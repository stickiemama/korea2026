The cards

About the crater

Weird but true

A crater with no mountain

Most of Jeju's craters sit on top of an oreum. Sangumburi is different: it's a big hole in fairly flat ground, with hardly any cone around it. Scientists think it formed in an explosive blast as gas and steam burst out, rather than from lava building a hill. It's one of the very few craters of this kind in Korea.

Nature

A forest in a bowl

Down in the crater, sheltered from the wind, grows a thick forest. The sunny and shady sides have different plants, from warm-climate evergreens to trees usually found in colder places, so researchers come here to study them. That's why it's a Natural Monument.

Name

What does Sangumburi mean?

Gumburi is Jeju dialect for a crater, and san means mountain, so the name is roughly "mountain crater."

Volcano link

Where does the rain go?

Even after the typhoon, the crater floor holds no lake. Jeju's cracked volcanic rock lets the rain drain straight away underground. Compare it with Hallasan's crater lake, Baengnokdam.
