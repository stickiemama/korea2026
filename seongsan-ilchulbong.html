The cards

Born in the sea

A volcano that erupted underwater

Several thousand years ago, magma rose under shallow sea. When red-hot magma hits cold seawater, it explodes into steam and shatters into ash. The wet ash piled up around the vent and built this cone, called a "tuff cone."

Look for: stripes in the cliffs. Each layer of ash is a record of one burst of the eruption.
Weird but true

It used to be an island

When it formed, Seongsan Ilchulbong stood alone in the sea. Over time, sand and sediment piled up and joined it to Jeju. The village of Seongsan now sits on that strip of land.

Name

Fortress Mountain, Sunrise Peak

Seongsan means "fortress mountain," because the jagged rocks around the crater rim look like castle walls. Ilchulbong means "sunrise peak," because it faces east and is one of the first places in Korea to catch the morning sun.

Look for: the ring of rocky spikes along the rim. People say there are 99 of them, like a crown.
Inside the crater

A giant grassy bowl

The crater is about 600 m wide, roughly the length of six football fields. It's carpeted in grass and shrubs. People used to grow crops, gather grass and graze horses inside it, until it became protected.

Try: from the viewing deck, work out where farmers would have walked down into the bowl.
Famous

World Heritage, with company

In 2007, UNESCO listed Seongsan Ilchulbong as World Natural Heritage, together with Hallasan and the lava tubes, including Manjanggul where you were this morning. All three belong to the same volcanic story.

Hidden history

Secret caves for suicide boats

Near the end of World War II, the Japanese navy dug about 18 caves into the base of the peak. They planned to hide small boats packed with explosives there and ram them into Allied ships if an invasion came. The war ended before they were used.

Look for: dark openings at the foot of the cliffs near the shore.
Sea

The haenyeo's home beach

The rocky shore at the foot of the peak is where Seongsan's haenyeo divers work. They sometimes give diving demonstrations and sell fresh seafood they've just caught. Listen for the whistle they make when they surface.

View

Spot the cow island

From the top, look north-east for Udo, a low, flat island. Its name means "cow island," because people thought its shape looks like a lying-down cow. Ferries leave from Seongsan port.

Festival

The biggest sunrise party in Korea

Every New Year, thousands of people climb in the dark for the first sunrise of the year during the Seongsan Sunrise Festival. There are fireworks and music, and fishing boats light up and sound their horns as the sun appears.
