The cards

About Bukchon

Name

The north village

Bukchon means "north village." It sits between Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung, and in Joseon times it was home to high-ranking officials and nobles who wanted to live near the king.

Architecture

How a hanok works

Hanok are built of wood, earth, stone and paper, with deep eaves that keep out summer sun but let in low winter light. Floors are heated by ondol, and houses face south with a hill behind, following the old idea of baesan imsu, mountains behind and water in front.

Roofs

Roofs that smile

Hanok roofs curve gently upwards at the corners, which makes heavy tiled roofs look light. The tiles at the edge often carry patterns or characters wishing for good fortune and long life.

Be kind

A village where people live

So many tourists visit that residents complained about noise and crowds. Since 2025, visits to the core lanes have been limited to daytime hours, with fines for visiting outside them. Keep voices low, and don't peek into gates or doors.
