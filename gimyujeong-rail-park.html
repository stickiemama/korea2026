The cards

About the rail park

History

A railway that retired

The rail bikes run on the old Gyeongchun Line, a railway from Seoul to Chuncheon built in the 1930s. In 2010 a new, faster double-track line opened, and the old single track was left empty, so it was turned into a ride instead of being torn up.

The course

River, tunnels and a train

The one-way course runs about 6 km from Gimyujeong Station to a rest stop by the Bukhan River, then a little "Romance Train" carries riders the last 2.5 km. A shuttle bus brings everyone back.

Weird but true

Tunnels that party

The old railway tunnels are now light shows: a pink tunnel, a bubble tunnel, a Milky Way tunnel and even a "club" tunnel with coloured disco lights and loud music.

Name

A station named after a writer

Gimyujeong Station is named after Kim Yu-jeong (1908–1937), a short-story writer born in this village. His funny, warm stories about country life, like Spring, Spring and Camellias, are still read in Korean schools. He died of illness at just 29.

The station was renamed after him in 2004, said to be the first station in Korea named after a person.

Science

Why rail bikes roll so easily

Steel wheels on steel rails have very little rolling resistance, much less than rubber tyres on a road. That's why one pair of legs can push a heavy rail bike with ease, and why trains are so energy efficient.
