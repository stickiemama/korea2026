About the falls

Rare

A waterfall by the sea

Jeongbang drops 23 m off a cliff onto a rocky shore just steps from the sea. Waterfalls that land so close to the ocean are rare, which makes it one of Jeju's most famous sights.

Legend

Seobul was here

China's first emperor, Qin Shi Huang, wanted to live forever. Legend says he sent a servant, Seobul (Xu Fu in Chinese), to find the herb of eternal youth on Hallasan. On his way home, Seobul carved "Seobul was here" on the cliff by the falls. The name Seogwipo is said to mean the harbour where he "went back west."
