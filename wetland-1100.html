Part 2

The cards

Name

Named after its height

The 1100 Road crosses Hallasan's western slopes, and its highest point is at about 1,100 m above sea level. The wetland and rest area there are simply called "1100 Highland."

Weird but true

A wetland on a leaky island

Jeju's cracked lava usually lets rain drain straight underground, so wetlands are rare. Here the ground is flatter and holds water, forming small pools, marshes and peat bogs. Fog and mist roll in so often that they help keep it wet.

Nature

Rare residents

The wetland is home to threatened birds and plants, and to animals and insects found only on Jeju. A wooden boardwalk lets visitors look without trampling the soft, spongy ground.

Global

A Ramsar wetland

In 2009 it was listed under the Ramsar Convention, an international agreement to protect important wetlands. The treaty is named after the city in Iran where it was signed in 1971. Wetlands store water, prevent floods and give homes to huge numbers of species.

Peat

Mud that stores history

Peat forms when dead plants pile up in waterlogged ground and rot very slowly. Layers of peat can trap pollen for thousands of years, so scientists can read them like a history book of which plants grew here.
