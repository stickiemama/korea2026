Part 2

The cards

Surf

Jeju's surf beach

Jungmun faces south into the open sea, so it catches bigger waves than most Jeju beaches. It's one of the best-known surf spots in Korea, with surf schools along the shore.

Shells

What was the conch?

A large spiral shell on a Jeju beach is very likely a sora, the horned turban snail. It's one of the main shellfish the haenyeo dive for, and you'll see it grilled or raw in seafood restaurants. Turban snails graze on seaweed with a tongue covered in tiny teeth.

Good to know: in Jeju, the haenyeo of each village look after the shellfish on their stretch of coast, so shells you find alive are best put back where you found them.
Name

Two names in one

Jungmun is the resort area and Saekdal is the old village neighbourhood the beach belongs to, so locals use both names together.

Nearby

Lava columns next door

Just along the coast are the hexagonal lava columns of Jusangjeolli Cliff, on our list for Friday. The dark cliffs around this beach are part of the same lava flows.
